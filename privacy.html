VE Cam NVR Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

VE Cam NVR is a companion application for compatible VE Cam network video recorder systems.

Data We Process

VE Cam NVR may process a device identifier and basic device/app information in order to provide App Trust, device authorization, NVR connectivity, and push notifications.

Firebase Cloud Messaging may process push notification tokens and technical device/app information required to deliver notifications and operate the notification service.

Limited diagnostic information may also be processed for app functionality and troubleshooting.

Camera and Recording Data

Camera video, recordings, snapshots, and NVR configuration remain associated with the user's VE Cam NVR system. VE Cam NVR does not operate an advertising network and does not sell camera footage or personal information.

Tracking and Advertising

VE Cam NVR does not use collected data for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Remote Access

VE Cam NVR can work with Tailscale for remote access. Tailscale is a separate service with its own privacy practices and policies.
